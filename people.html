About

Dr. Yunli Shao is an Assistant Professor at the University of Georgia College of Engineering. His expertise is in real-time modeling, control, and analysis of dynamical systems, including model-based and learning-based optimal control, real-time optimization, and estimation and prediction under uncertainty. The primary application area is vehicle and transportation systems, spanning powertrain and vehicle dynamics, automated and connected vehicles, traffic simulation, and efficient operation at the system level. His work also includes the development of anything-in-the-loop (XIL) methodology and platforms that surround real controllers and hardware with realistic simulated traffic environments for accelerated control development and training.
