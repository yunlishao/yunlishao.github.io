Research

Real-Time Optimal Control of Connected and Automated Vehicles

Connectivity tells an automated vehicle what the signals and traffic ahead will do. We design controllers that use this preview to drive more efficiently and smoothly, account for how each powertrain uses energy, and run fast enough for real time.
